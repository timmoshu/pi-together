# Code of conduct

Be respectful, specific, and constructive. Welcome people regardless of identity or experience. Focus
criticism on the work, assume good faith while verifying security claims, and avoid harassment,
threats, discrimination, sexualized conduct, doxxing, or disclosure of confidential information.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and temporarily or permanently restrict participation.
Report conduct concerns through a private maintainer channel, not a public issue. Reports will be
handled as confidentially as practical, with conflicts of interest delegated to another maintainer.
